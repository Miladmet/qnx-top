Purpose-Built for QNX Microkernels

Traditional Unix tools can't see microkernel primitives. qnx-top bridges the gap.

🔀

REPLY-Blocked Client Tracking

Shows which threads are REPLY-blocked, on which server process and channel, and for how long. Priority inversion detection is not implemented.

🛡️

Adaptive Partitioning (APS)

Detects whether the APS scheduler is active on the target. Per-partition budgets are not read yet and are shown as N/A.

📂

/dev Listing and Telemetry Endpoint

Lists the entries under /dev (owner and handler details are shown as N/A) and serves a text report at /dev/qnx_top.
